WakeBee 0.15.6

Clearer screen controls and more compact settings.

  • Keep display awake is now called Keep screen on, with a short recommendation for computer use.
  • Keep screen on starts enabled for new installations. Existing saved choices are preserved.
  • Descriptions sit closer to their controls throughout General, Closed Lid, and License settings.
  • Closed-lid help explains when to keep the screen on, lid open, and Mac unlocked for apps that click or type.

End your session and keep the lid open during installation. Versions 0.14 and earlier require a one-time manual migration.
