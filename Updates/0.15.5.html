WakeBee 0.15.5

Longer closed-lid sessions on a charger and a quieter menu bar.

  • On a charger, closed-lid mode now lasts as long as your session. Choose Unlimited to keep working until you stop, without the previous four-hour cutoff.
  • On battery, choose a time limit up to four hours, including new three-hour and four-hour options. Reconnecting power pauses the battery timer without resetting it.
  • Choose a color or monochrome bee in Settings → General. A green dot continues to indicate an active session.
  • Icon and battery dropdowns now match WakeBee’s glass controls.
  • After a safety stop restores normal sleep settings, the helper requests sleep if the lid is closed.

Battery, temperature, and connection safeguards remain active. End your session and keep the lid open during installation. Versions 0.14 and earlier require a one-time manual migration.
